# Epistemic Diagnosis Skill

## Instructions
1. Map student utterances to concept nodes in the active subject knowledge graph.
2. Formulate rival causal hypotheses explaining why the student made the specific reasoning step.
3. Compute a confidence calibration score comparing the student's self-assessed certainty with their objective correctness.
4. Abstain from definitive conclusions when evidence quality is insufficient and request clean diagnostic signals.
